## Expected production causal chain

The evidence target is:

`hot pressurized Water/Steam chamber -> Pressure propagation -> Wood relief-seam stress -> rupture -> persistent opening -> Steam/gas vent to exterior -> chamber Pressure relief`

Production pass order means rupture occurs after the current tick's movement; vent movement can begin on later ticks. Pressure is spatial rather than Matter-owned, and vacated Empty cells are cleared by the production pressure path.

Both seam inner faces already touch P=180, above the Wood rupture threshold, so early milestones may share tick 1. The top seam also begins at T=95, above the Wood ignition threshold. Top Wood loss or Smoke alone is therefore not sufficient rupture attribution. The Harness records the cold bottom seam, opening persistence, outside-chamber Steam, and chamber Pressure response separately.

## Measurement boundary

- Chamber Pressure uses the fixed cavity mask `[40,216) x [46,216)`, including zero-pressure cells after evacuation.
- Outside-chamber Steam means cells outside `[32,224) x [38,224)`; Steam inside a seam volume is not yet exterior venting.
- A persistent opening is a baseline seam cell that remains non-Wood for the configured diagnostic streak; it need not remain Empty while Steam occupies it.
- The opening streak includes the tick-1 diagnostic. A confirmed opening that later returns to zero open seam cells is an explicit hard failure and receives an `opening-reseal` evidence badge.
- Causal vent evidence requires persistent-opening confirmation followed by Steam in an authored seam and exterior Steam at the same or a later diagnostic sample. The Harness does not invent sub-sample ordering when both observations first occur together.
- `pressure_active_cells` measures the production activity mask and is not a substitute for absolute chamber Pressure.
- Chamber mean/max values are canonicalized to the same nine-decimal values written to telemetry before peak, relief, and runaway decisions; the independent coordinator rejects a one-unit (`1e-9`) recorded drift.
- Pressure need not reach zero and the whole world need not reach all-sleep.
- Long Pressure/Thermal tails, remaining terminal activity, a persistent plume, or only one ruptured seam are human-review conditions, not automatic failure by themselves. Runnable chunks alone do not impose an all-sleep requirement.
