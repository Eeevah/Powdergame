# Powdergame First Interaction Roster

## Status

- Type: `DERIVED`
- Authority: non-authoritative content selection
- Purpose: reduce the broad encyclopedia into a compact first interaction-heavy roster.
- **This does not expand M0, register new runtime Materials, or change Evidence Gates.**

> **물질 수보다 서로 만났을 때 생기는 변화의 수와 질을 우선한다.**

Selection order:

```text
real high-interaction matter
→ historical / alchemical framing
→ real exotic matter
→ only then original gap-filler
```

The first broad target roster is **38 Matter identities including the current 16 foundation/catalog-direction entries**.

## 2. Real-world interaction core — 15

### Dirt

**Dictionary:** 평범한 흙이지만 물과 생명을 만나면 세계의 표면이 바뀐다.

**Why:** connects Water, Seed, Plant, drying and terrain.

```text
Dirt + Water → Mud-like state/material
Dirt + Seed + Water → growth substrate
Heat / drying → loose Dirt
```

### Clay

**Dictionary:** 젖으면 빚을 수 있고, 구우면 다시 흙으로 돌아가지 않는 재료가 된다.

**Why:** one of the clearest bridges from natural Matter to manufacturing.

```text
Clay + Water → workable wet clay
Wet Clay + Heat → Brick
```

### Brick

**Dictionary:** 불이 흙에게 준 기억. 반응의 결과가 곧 벽이 된다.

**Why:** visible reward for the Clay chain; introduces manufactured STATIC Matter without a crafting menu.

### Coal

**Dictionary:** 검은 돌 속에 오래 묵은 불. 태우면 Heat와 Smoke를 남긴다.

**Why:** fuel, smelting, Ash/Smoke and explosive-production hub.

`Charcoal` should initially be a variant/alias unless later gameplay clearly distinguishes it.

### Sulfur

**Dictionary:** 노란 광물 하나가 불, 냄새, 화약과 옛 연금술을 한꺼번에 불러온다.

**Why:** strong real identity plus Gunpowder production and alchemical symbolism.

### Saltpeter / Nitrate Salt

**Dictionary:** 평범한 흰 가루지만 연료와 황을 만나면 불의 속도를 바꾼다.

**Why:** turns Gunpowder into a discoverable production chain instead of an isolated magic explosive.

### Gunpowder

**Dictionary:** 조용히 쌓이는 가루가 불씨 하나로 압력의 폭풍이 된다.

**Why:** one of the highest interaction-yield Materials in the whole corpus.

```text
Powder movement
+ ignition
→ rapid combustion
→ Heat + Pressure
→ Smoke / residue
```

No bespoke radial explosion solver should be required.

### Alcohol

**Dictionary:** 물과 섞이는 액체인데 불과도 금방 친구가 된다.

**Why:** gives a flammable liquid behavior distinct from Oil.

```text
Alcohol + Water → mixing/dilution behavior
Alcohol + sufficient Heat → combustion
```

### Methane

**Dictionary:** 보이지 않는 빈 방이 사실은 연료로 차 있을 수 있다는 걸 가르치는 가스.

**Why:** makes confinement and ventilation meaningful.

```text
GAS accumulation
→ ignition
→ Heat + Pressure
```

### Dry Ice

**Dictionary:** 녹지 않는 얼음. 따뜻해지면 물이 아니라 무거운 숨이 된다.

**Why:** introduces sublimation with ordinary transition grammar.

```text
Dry Ice + Heat → CO2 GAS
```

### CO2

**Dictionary:** 불이 남긴 무거운 숨. 낮은 곳에 고이고 불꽃을 약하게 만든다.

**Why:** heavy GAS + combustion suppression; pairs naturally with Dry Ice.

### Oxygen

**Dictionary:** 스스로 타지는 않지만 다른 불에게 먹이를 준다.

**Why:** an interaction amplifier rather than another fuel.

Oxygen must not automatically require full atmospheric-composition simulation.

### Brine

**Dictionary:** 소금이 물 속에서 사라진 뒤에도 물의 성격을 바꿔 놓는다.

**Why:** links Salt, Water, Ice and later Metal corrosion.

```text
Salt + Water → Brine
Brine → altered freezing behavior
Brine + Metal → corrosion candidate
```

### Mercury

**Dictionary:** 금속인데 흐른다. 다른 액체 아래로 가라앉으며 금속의 상식을 깨뜨린다.

**Why:** liquid-metal identity, extreme density ordering, metallurgy/alchemy connection.

### Obsidian

**Dictionary:** 용암이 너무 빨리 식어서 돌보다 유리에 가까워진 검은 상처.

**Why:** highly readable Lava + rapid-cooling result.

```text
Lava + rapid cooling → Obsidian
```

## 3. Real exotic / science-rich core — 3

These are real or reality-anchored but feel sufficiently strange to create discovery without fictional Matter.

### Methane Clathrate

**Dictionary:** 얼음처럼 보이지만 불을 품고 있다. 따뜻해지면 감춰 둔 메탄을 토해낸다.

```text
Heat
→ Methane release
→ confinement
→ ignition
→ Pressure
→ rupture / vent
```

**Why:** perhaps the strongest single real-world causal-chain Material beyond the foundation.

### Perchlorate Dust

**Dictionary:** 평범한 화성 먼지처럼 보이지만 충분히 가열하면 주변 불이 갑자기 살아난다.

**Why:** creates a delayed oxidizer behavior while also giving space/regolith worlds a meaningful hazard.

Do not model exact Martian chemistry; retain the intuitive mechanism.

### Aerogel

**Dictionary:** 거의 공기처럼 가벼운 벽. 열은 막지만 충격까지 막지는 못한다.

**Why:** insulation with a clear counter/failure mode instead of another invincible wall.

```text
very low thermal transfer
+ mechanical fragility / pressure weakness
```

## 4. Original gap-fillers — only 4

The first roster deliberately keeps fictional Matter rare.

Each one must fill an interaction verb that the real set does not express cleanly enough.

### Pyrostor

**Gap:** delayed thermal storage/discharge.

**Dictionary:** 열을 잊지 않는 돌. 조금씩 삼키다가 한계를 넘으면 기억한 열을 되돌린다.

```text
Heat absorption
→ limited capacity
→ threshold
→ delayed Heat / Pressure release
```

### Phase-Wax

**Gap:** movable latent-heat storage.

**Dictionary:** 녹는 동안 열을 숨기고, 다시 굳을 때 그 열을 돌려주는 왁스.

```text
solid ↔ liquid
→ absorb / release Heat
```

Prefer phase behavior over a universal extra per-cell energy ledger.

### Heat-Diode Material

Working name only; final original name should be decided later.

**Gap:** directional thermal construction.

**Dictionary:** 한쪽에서는 열을 받아들이지만 반대쪽에서는 문을 닫는 판.

```text
strong heat transfer direction A→B
weak transfer B→A
```

This is justified only if directional material orientation can be represented cheaply.

### Vapor-Latch

**Gap:** gas storage with conditional release.

**Dictionary:** 증기를 삼키고 있다가 압력이 풀리는 순간 한꺼번에 숨을 내쉰다.

```text
Steam / GAS capture
→ finite capacity
→ pressure condition
→ release
```

Implementation must avoid automatically adding generic capacity bytes to every cell.

## 7. Merge / alias policy

Do not spend a Material identity on small variations too early.

Examples:

```text
Charcoal → Coal variant until distinct behavior is justified
Molten Metal → Metal transition/state unless a separate identity is required by implementation
Wet Clay → staged Clay identity/state
Mud → Dirt + Water result; separate Matter only if movement/behavior clearly earns it
Poison → family/tag; add concrete toxic substances later
multiple acids → Acid family until different reaction verbs justify separation
multiple fictional super-metals → reference quarry only
```

A new name is not a new gameplay verb.

Keep in encyclopedia/research but outside the first interaction roster:

- observation-dependent behavior

- gravity rewrite
- Light-only decorative Matter

These may later become Field / Agent / Structure / Concept / Meta systems rather than Matter.

## 9. Why 38 is enough

The roster is intentionally small enough that each identity can be learned, but broad enough to create many causal chains.

```text
38 Matter identities
× Temperature
× Pressure
× Density
× Movement
× Combustion
× Transition
× Growth
× Manufacturing
```

The target is not 38 isolated recipes. The target is a world where the same Matter participates in several chains.

Representative chains:

```text
Clay + Water + Heat → Brick

Sulfur + Coal + Nitrate → Gunpowder
Gunpowder + Heat → Pressure + Smoke

Dry Ice + Heat → CO2
CO2 pocket + Fire → combustion suppressed

Clathrate + Heat → Methane
Methane + confinement + ignition → Pressure → rupture → vent

Salt + Water → Brine
Brine + cold → altered freezing
Brine + Metal → corrosion candidate

Lava + Water → Steam + Pressure
Lava + rapid cooling → Obsidian

Pyrostor + Heat → stored danger
stored Pyrostor + later trigger → Heat / Pressure release
```

If an entry does not create chains of this kind, it should remain in the encyclopedia rather than enter the runtime roster.

---

## 10. Decision rule for future additions

A future candidate enters the roster only when at least one is true:

1. it adds a distinct local verb;
2. it closes a meaningful interaction gap;
3. it connects several existing families;
4. it creates a clearly readable production/decay chain;
5. it provides a counter/failure mechanism missing from the current world.

Otherwise preserve it as encyclopedia knowledge, reference, variant or alias.
