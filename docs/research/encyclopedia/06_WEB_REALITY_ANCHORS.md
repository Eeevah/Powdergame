## Mars perchlorate / Perchlorate Dust

NASA reports from Phoenix/Curiosity-related work support perchlorate or oxychlorine salts in Martian soil and oxygen release during heating/decomposition. Powdergame translation: a boring-looking regolith additive that becomes an oxidizer only after sufficient heat.

- https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20090010422
- https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20140012600

**Dictionary line:** “평범한 화성 먼지 속에 숨은 산소 빚. 데우는 순간 주변 불이 갑자기 살아난다.”

## Methane hydrate / Clathrate

USGS describes gas hydrate as an ice-like crystalline water+gas solid containing large amounts of methane; warming can release methane, hence the familiar ‘ice that burns’ demonstration. Powdergame translation: Heat → gas release → confinement → ignition → Pressure.

- https://www.usgs.gov/faqs/what-are-gas-hydrates
- https://www.usgs.gov/media/images/burning-gas-hydrate

**Dictionary line:** “얼음처럼 보이지만 불을 품고 있다. 따뜻해지는 순간 감춰 둔 메탄을 토해낸다.”

## Titan hydrocarbon lakes / Tholin Tar

NASA Cassini material describes stable Titan surface liquids as methane/ethane rather than water, while atmospheric chemistry produces complex organic aerosols commonly described as tholin-like material. Powdergame translation: ‘false water’ lakes plus organic tar/sediment weathering.

- https://science.nasa.gov/mission/cassini/science/titan/
- https://science.nasa.gov/resource/titans-northern-lakes-salt-flats/
- https://science.nasa.gov/solar-system/revealing-titans-rugged-surface/

**Dictionary line:** “비처럼 내리고 호수를 만들지만 물은 아니다. 불을 만나기 전까지는 낯선 바다처럼 보인다.”

## Aerogel / Thermal-Felt inspiration

NASA describes silica aerogel as extremely porous, very low-density material with exceptionally low thermal conductivity, while also noting brittleness and the need for reinforcement in practical uses. Powdergame translation: excellent insulation that pays for itself with fragility/compression failure instead of absolute immunity.

- https://spinoff.nasa.gov/Spinoff2010/cg_2.html
- https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20110012915

**Dictionary line:** “거의 공기처럼 가벼운 벽. 열은 멈추지만 충격까지 멈추지는 못한다.”

## Shape-memory alloy / Memory-Alloy inspiration

NASA describes shape-memory alloys as metals that recover a trained shape through solid-state phase transformation when temperature changes. Powdergame translation: staged Material identity can encode memory without a universal per-cell shape vector.

- https://www.nasa.gov/aeronautics/memory-metals-are-shaping-the-evolution-of-aviation/
- https://technology.nasa.gov/patent/LEW-TOPS-130

**Dictionary line:** “구부러져도 잊지 않는 금속. 열은 힘이 아니라 ‘원래 모습’을 불러오는 신호다.”

## Piezoelectric material / Fulgur-Crust inspiration

NIST summarizes piezoelectrics as materials that convert mechanical stress to electricity and can convert electrical input back to mechanical response. Powdergame translation: Pressure pulse ↔ Electric signal once an electricity family is justified.

- https://www.nist.gov/news-events/news/2014/01/nearly-everyone-uses-piezoelectrics-be-nice-know-how-they-work

**Dictionary line:** “돌을 누르면 번개가 되고, 번개를 먹이면 다시 돌이 움직인다.”

## Superionic ice

LLNL experimentally reported superionic water at giant-planet conditions: a solid oxygen lattice with highly mobile hydrogen ions and very high ionic conductivity. Powdergame should not copy the extreme real pressure/temperature directly; it is a powerful visual/mechanical anchor for a ‘solid that carries ions like a liquid.’

- https://www.llnl.gov/article/44081/first-experimental-evidence-superionic-ice
- https://www.llnl.gov/article/45336/giant-lasers-crystallize-water-shockwaves-revealing-atomic-structure-superionic-ice

**Dictionary line:** “겉은 얼음인데 안에서는 수소가 액체처럼 달린다. 고체와 전해질의 경계가 무너진 얼음.”
